## §0 At a glance

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| individuals per diagnostic run | 10,000 |
| age bins `T` | 20 (two-year, ages 50–90) |
| observed channels `C` | 12 — upstream 0–3, midstream 4–7, downstream 8–11 |
| latent streams | 3 (U upstream, M midstream, D downstream) |
| static covariates `P` | 3 (birth cohort, sex, baseline frailty) |
| scenarios | 6 — accumulation, critical period, sensitive period, chain of risk, mobility, null |
| effect size, all scenarios | `log(1.5)` per within-bin SD of the history score |
| target disease CIF at `T` | 0.15 — expected achieved to 1e-8 |
| target mortality CIF at `T` | 0.20 — expected achieved to 1e-8 |
| target attrition before event | 0.30 |
| observed cells | ~54.5% — the remaining ~45.5% is missing by design |
| master seed | 20260724 |
| runtime, six scenarios | ~8 s total |

**The one fact that must not be missed.** *Roughly 45% of the observed data matrix is
missing, and that is the point, not a defect.* Three mechanisms stack — 10% item
non-response everywhere, a midstream block observed only on even bins and then only for a
rotating half-sample, and monotone informative dropout calibrated to 30%. Every
observation-rate figure in the reports is read wrong if this is read as data loss: it is
the mask the mask-aware architecture exists to handle.

## §1 Latent trajectories

Three AR(1) streams per individual, generated by `_generate_latent`:

```
U_t = rho_u·U_{t-1}             + xi_u + eps          rho_u = 0.9
M_t = a·U_{t-1} + rho_m·M_{t-1} + xi_m + eps          a = 0.4, rho_m = 0.7
D_t = b·M_{t-1} + c·U_{t-1} + rho_d·D_{t-1} + xi_d + eps   b = 0.4, c = 0.2, rho_d = 0.8
```

Individual random intercepts `xi ~ N(0, 0.5)`, innovations `eps ~ N(0, 1)`. The direct
`c` path means mediation is partial, not complete.

Observed channels load onto their stream: `X[c,t] = lambda_c · L_stream(c)[t] + nu`, with
`lambda_c ~ U(0.6, 1.0)` and `nu ~ N(0, 0.5)`. **Channels within a stream are exchangeable
by construction** — that is deliberate, so the specification's permutation test tests the
architecture and not the DGP.

`test_latent_mediation_coefficients_recoverable_by_ols` recovers `a`, `b`, `c`, and all
three `rho` by OLS on the latent series to within 0.02, which is what establishes that the
generator implements the equations above rather than something adjacent to them.

**Decisions baked in:** the exchangeability requirement is a property of the source
specification, not a pipeline choice.

## §2 Scenario history scores

`_scenario_score` maps the latent history to a per-bin risk score and, simultaneously, to
the stream-level temporal support that becomes ground truth.

| scenario | `f(history up to t)` | support |
|---|---|---|
| accumulation | `mean(U_s : s ≤ t)` | uniform over all 20 bins |
| critical period | `mean(U_s : s ∈ [5,8])`, zero before bin 5 | bins 5–8 only |
| sensitive period | Gaussian-weighted mean of `U`, centred at bin 6, SD 2 | the Gaussian weights |
| chain of risk | `mean(U_1..6) × mean(D_10..14)`, zero before bin 10 | upstream 1–6 **and** downstream 10–14 |
| mobility | slope of `U` orthogonalised against its level | `abs` of the residualised slope weights |
| null | identically zero | exactly zero |

Every non-constant score is then standardised **within each bin** and multiplied by
`log(1.5)`, so one coefficient means the same thing in all five non-null scenarios
(**D02**). Bins whose SD falls below `1e-8` are left at zero rather than divided through.

**Decisions baked in:** D02.

## §3 Hazards and competing-risk calibration

Disease and death are drawn from a per-bin multinomial over `{no event, disease, death}`:

- disease linear predictor: `beta·score + gamma'S`, `gamma = [0.15, 0.25, 0.50]`
- death linear predictor: `0.405·frailty + 0.223·cumulative_downstream`

Both intercepts are solved by **alternating bisection** — 8 outer rounds of 28 bisection
steps each — because the two causes share an event-free survival term and are therefore
not separable (**D03**). `_expected_cif` computes the closed-form joint CIF at each step,
so calibration never depends on sampling.

Static covariates are standardised. `_sigmoid` is written in two branches to avoid
overflow on large negative linear predictors, and the exponent is clipped to ±40.

**Decisions baked in:** D03.

## §4 Informative attrition

Dropout hazard is `sigmoid(intercept + 1.0 · standardised_disease_LP)`, restricted to
bins before the individual's event, with the intercept bisected to 30% cumulative
dropout. Dropout is therefore **informative**: people at higher disease risk leave
earlier.

Covariates go missing from the dropout bin onward, **but outcome follow-up continues** —
event times and types remain observed after dropout. This is the single most
misread property of the simulation; it is stated on every report page for that reason.

## §5 The missingness mask

`_apply_missingness` stacks three mechanisms in this fixed order:

1. 10% item non-response, independent across every cell.
2. Midstream channels 4–7: never observed on odd bins; on even bins observed only for a
   rotating half-sample, drawn independently per bin and **shared across all four
   midstream channels** within that bin.
3. Everything from the dropout bin onward is masked.

`Delta` records, per cell, the number of bins since that channel was last observed —
the input a mask-aware model consumes alongside `X` and `M`. Masked entries of `X` are
`NaN`, and `test_shapes_reproducibility_and_missingness_order` asserts that the `NaN`
pattern is exactly `~M`.

**Property of the source vs. the pipeline:** the rotating half-sample is a design feature
of the simulated *study*, chosen to mimic HRS-style rotating modules. It is not an
artifact of how the data is processed.

## §6 Ground truth

`_channel_weight_map` turns stream-level support into the `12 × 20` map `w_true` by
splitting each stream's support across its four channels in proportion to their sampled
loadings, then normalising to sum to 1 (**D04**).

This is *structural support at the population level*. It is not an individual gradient,
and in the multiplicative chain-of-risk scenario individual gradients genuinely differ in
magnitude and sign — a fixed map cannot represent that, which is why per-individual
perturbation detection is retained as a separate later endpoint rather than folded in
here.

**Decisions baked in:** D04.

## §7 Reproducibility

`(master_seed, scenario, N, replicate)` determines the output completely. The seed is the
first 32 bits of SHA-256 over `"master_seed|scenario|N|replicate"` — chosen over Python's
`hash()`, which is randomised per process and would make a regenerated figure silently
differ from the one in a report. `MASTER_SEED = 20260724` is a **named default argument**
of `generate_dataset`, overridable per call.

Every run writes `output/tables/dgp_manifest_YYYYMMDD.json` carrying the git hash, the
master seed, the full config, and per-scenario realized figures.

## Limitations — what these numbers cannot support

- **Per-bin standardisation is a design device.** It equalises signal strength across
  scenarios so one coefficient is comparable. It is not a proposed epidemiological
  estimand and should not be described as one.
- **`w_true` is population-level.** It cannot be used to claim anything about an
  individual's attribution, particularly under chain of risk.
- **Realized ≠ expected.** Realized incidences at `N = 10,000` sit within ~0.008 of
  target; that spread is Monte Carlo error. A single run's realized figure is not
  evidence that calibration drifted — check the expected CIF in the manifest, which is
  exact to 1e-8.
- **No model has been fitted.** Nothing here speaks to whether any architecture recovers
  this structure. That is the next deliverable and is blocked on review of the DGP.
- **Nothing here has touched HRS.** The DGP is calibrated to plausible targets, not
  fitted to real data.
